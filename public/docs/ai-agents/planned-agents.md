# Planned Agents

These agents are planned for future versions. They should follow the same rule as MVP agents: AI suggests, humans decide.

## Job Post Generator

Planned integration: Recruiter creates or edits a Job Post.

Expected benefit:

- Draft public job post text from the approved Job Request.
- Save recruiter writing time.
- Keep job post text aligned with internal requirements.

Expected human review:

- Recruiter edits and publishes the job post.

## Feedback Summary

Planned integration: After interview feedback is submitted.

Expected benefit:

- Summarize multiple interviewer notes.
- Highlight common strengths and concerns.
- Help Recruiter or Hiring Manager review faster.

Expected human review:

- Recruiter or Hiring Manager reviews before moving the candidate forward.

## Pipeline Next-Step Agent

Planned integration: Candidate Pipeline and application status changes.

Expected benefit:

- Suggest next action.
- Surface blockers.
- Warn about aging tasks or pending feedback.

Expected human review:

- Owner decides and executes the next step.
