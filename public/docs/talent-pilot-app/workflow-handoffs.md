# Workflow Handoffs

Talent Pilot moves a hiring request from one role to the next. Each role owns one part of the work, then passes it forward when ready.

## Simple Flow

1. **Presales** creates the Job Request.
2. **PMO** reviews the request and checks whether an internal bench employee can fill it.
3. **Recruiter** sources candidates if the role must be hired externally.
4. **Interviewers or HODs** interview candidates and submit feedback.
5. **Hiring Manager** reviews the final candidate evidence and records the outcome.
6. **Recruiter or Hiring Manager** closes the position when the hiring outcome is complete.

## Role Responsibilities

| Role | Main Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Presales | Explains the need: role, client, skills, timing, and why the position is needed. |
| PMO | Reviews the request, checks internal bench options, and forwards the work to recruiters when hiring is needed. |
| Recruiter | Publishes jobs, sources candidates, screens applications, and moves candidates through stages. |
| Interviewer | Completes assigned interview rounds and submits feedback. |
| HOD | Handles department or final technical review when the process requires it. |
| Hiring Manager | Reviews final evidence, decides the outcome, and helps close the request. |
| Tenant Admin | Configures users, roles, departments, workflows, and system settings. Admins do not normally own daily hiring decisions. |

## How Interviews Are Scheduled

Recruiter schedules interviews from the candidate application workflow.

The schedule includes:

- Interview round.
- Interviewer or HOD.
- Date and time.
- Meeting link or location.
- Notes for the interviewer.

After the interview is scheduled, the assigned interviewer sees it in **Interview Feedback**. The interviewer can use AI question recommendations, complete the interview, and submit structured feedback.

## How A Candidate Moves Forward

Recruiter reviews application evidence and interview feedback after each stage.

The candidate can be:

- Moved to the next interview round.
- Put on hold.
- Rejected.
- Forwarded to Hiring Manager Review.

AI can help summarize or explain evidence, but the recruiter and hiring users make the decision.

## How Positions Get Closed

A position is closed when the hiring need is resolved.

Common closure paths:

- A candidate is hired and the request is fulfilled.
- The selected candidate does not join and the team records the final outcome.
- The business no longer needs the role.
- The request is cancelled or closed by an authorized user.

When a position is closed, Talent Pilot keeps the history: request details, candidate stages, interviews, feedback, decisions, and audit records.
