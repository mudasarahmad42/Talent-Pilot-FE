# Recruitment Queue

Recruitment Queue helps recruiters manage sourcing-ready work, publish jobs, review candidate recommendations, and move candidates into screening.

## Queue Purpose

The queue should show job requests that require recruiter action.

Examples:

- A request is approved and ready for sourcing.
- A published job has new applications.
- Talent rediscovery produced warm candidate matches.
- AI headhunting produced online leads.
- Candidate invitations need review or sending.

## Sourcing Workspace

The recruiter sourcing page brings together request details, job post status, matching evidence, candidates, and outreach actions.

Recommended sections:

- Request summary
- Job post editor
- Interview template selection
- Talent rediscovery results
- AI headhunting leads
- Candidate invitations
- Application list

## Job Publishing

Recruiters can draft, publish, close, and update job posts based on permission and assignment ownership.

Candidate-facing job posts should be created from internal job requests but remain separate from the request itself.

## Talent Rediscovery

Talent rediscovery ranks active tenant candidates with previous useful application history.

Rules:

- Ranking is advisory.
- Recruiters decide who to invite.
- Evidence should explain why a candidate is relevant.
- Candidate personal identifiers should not be searched externally.

## Candidate Invitations

Recruiters can select candidates and send invitation emails for a published post. Invitation links should help candidates reach the job detail or apply flow but should not bypass login or application validation.
