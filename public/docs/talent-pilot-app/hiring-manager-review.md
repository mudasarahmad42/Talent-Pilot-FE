# Hiring Manager Review

Hiring Manager Review supports final candidate evaluation, offer decisions, and request closure after recruiting and interview work has produced enough evidence.

## Review Purpose

Hiring managers need a concise view of candidate evidence without browsing every operational screen.

The review page should show:

- Candidate profile summary
- Job and request context
- Interview feedback
- Decision brief
- Offer draft context when available
- Meeting and outcome history

## Decision Evidence

Decision evidence should be clear and traceable.

Sources may include:

- Recruiter screening notes
- Interviewer feedback
- CV and profile evidence
- AI decision brief
- Hiring manager notes

AI-generated summaries are advisory and should never make the final hiring decision automatically.

## Final Outcomes

Typical outcomes:

- Advance
- Offer
- Hold
- Reject
- Candidate declined
- Joined
- Request closed

Outcome actions should require permission and should record the acting user and timestamp.

## Offer Discussion

Hiring managers may schedule offer presentation meetings or follow-up discussions. Candidate-facing notifications should contain only appropriate meeting details.

## Closing The Request

When hiring is complete or cancelled, the Hiring Manager or Tenant Admin can close the job request with a required business reason.

Closure should preserve:

- Reason
- Final status
- Acting user
- Date and time
- Candidate outcome context when relevant
