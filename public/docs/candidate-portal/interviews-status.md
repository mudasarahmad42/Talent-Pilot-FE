# Interviews and Status

Candidate Portal should help candidates understand where they are in the hiring process without exposing internal workflow details.

## Application Status

Application status should be clear and candidate-safe.

Candidate-facing examples:

- Submitted
- Under review
- Interview scheduled
- Feedback pending
- Offer discussion
- Not selected
- Closed

Internal labels, assignment queues, and AI ranking details should stay inside Talent Pilot App.

## Interview Schedule

When interviews are scheduled, candidates should see:

- Interview round name
- Date and time
- Interview format
- Meeting location or link when available
- Preparation notes
- Contact or support details

Calendar integration may provide meeting metadata, but Candidate Portal should still show a reliable fallback.

## Notifications

Candidate notifications can be sent by email and reflected in the portal.

Common notifications:

- Application received
- Interview scheduled
- Interview updated
- Offer discussion scheduled
- Application closed

Messages should be respectful and avoid internal operational language.

## Status Timeline

The status page can show a simple timeline:

1. Application submitted.
2. Screening review.
3. Interview process.
4. Hiring manager review.
5. Final outcome.

The timeline should show progress without revealing private feedback or decision notes.

## Support

If a candidate has a scheduling or document issue, Candidate Portal should guide them to a clear support path rather than exposing internal users or workflow queues.
