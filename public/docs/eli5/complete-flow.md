# ELI5 Complete Flow

Talent Pilot helps a company move from "we need someone for this work" to "we have selected the right candidate" without losing track of who did what.

The simple rule is:

**AI helps explain, draft, rank, and summarize. People still review, approve, reject, hire, and communicate.**

## What To Click First

If you are using the demo role cards, start with the role that matches the work you want to do.

| Role card | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| Presales | Start a new job request for a client or internal need. |
| PMO / Resource Manager | Check whether an internal bench employee can fill the request. |
| Recruiter / HR | Source candidates, publish jobs, review applications, and schedule interviews. |
| Interviewer | Give scores, notes, and a recommendation after an interview. |
| Hiring Manager | Review the final candidate evidence and record the final outcome. |
| Admin | Configure users, departments, skills, workflows, email, calendar, and AI settings. |

## The Whole Story

### 1. Presales creates the request

Presales clicks the Presales card and creates a job request. They enter the role, client, department, location, skills, experience, and why the person is needed.

If the request text is rough, the AI draft button can turn the input into a cleaner job description. Presales still reviews and edits it before saving.

### 2. The request goes to PMO

After the request is submitted, PMO sees it in their queue. PMO checks whether the company already has an internal employee who could be a good fit.

This protects the business from hiring externally when a suitable bench employee may already exist.

### 3. PMO checks bench matches

PMO opens the review page and uses bench matching. Talent Pilot compares the request with internal employees using skills, role, experience, availability, project evidence, and matching notes.

The system can rank possible employees, but PMO decides what to do. PMO can recommend an internal person, ask questions, or route the request forward.

### 4. Recruiter starts sourcing if needed

If the request still needs external hiring, it moves to the recruiter. The recruiter reviews the request and opens the sourcing workspace.

From there, the recruiter can:

- Rediscover previous candidates already known to the company.
- Use AI headhunting to find lead-only public profiles when enabled.
- Publish the job.
- Add or invite candidates.
- Move candidates through the pipeline.

### 5. Candidates apply

Candidates use the candidate portal. They can view the job, submit an application, upload a CV, and later check their application status.

When a CV is uploaded, the system can help read it and prepare candidate profile information. A recruiter still reviews the candidate before taking action.

### 6. Recruiter reviews applicants

Recruiters compare applicants against the job request. Talent Pilot can rank applicants and explain why someone looks strong or weak.

The explanation is meant to help the recruiter understand the match. It is not an automatic hiring decision.

### 7. Interviews happen

Recruiters schedule interview rounds. Interviewers complete their feedback forms after each interview.

Interview feedback can include:

- Technical score.
- Communication score.
- Culture score.
- Recommendation, such as proceed or reject.
- Written notes.

The interview history stays attached to the application so later reviewers can see the evidence.

### 8. Hiring manager makes the final call

When the candidate reaches final review, the hiring manager sees the application, interview feedback, scores, notes, and decision summary.

The hiring manager records the final outcome, such as selected, rejected, on hold, or offer-related next steps.

### 9. Candidate sees status

The candidate portal shows the candidate what happened in plain language. The timeline shows application progress, interview updates, and final outcome.

If the candidate is rejected, the page should clearly show that status without making it look like a celebration.

## Where AI Helps

| Place | AI helps by |
| --- | --- |
| Job request | Drafting and cleaning up the request text. |
| PMO review | Ranking internal bench employees and explaining the match. |
| Recruiter sourcing | Ranking previous candidates and finding public leads when enabled. |
| CV review | Reading CV information into candidate fields for review. |
| Interviews | Suggesting structured interview questions. |
| Final review | Summarizing evidence for the hiring manager. |
| Copilot panels | Answering questions using visible workflow evidence. |

## What People Still Control

People remain in charge of important decisions.

- Presales decides whether the request is accurate.
- PMO decides whether an internal employee should be recommended.
- Recruiters decide who to contact, invite, reject, or move forward.
- Interviewers decide their own scores and notes.
- Hiring managers decide the final hiring outcome.
- Admins decide which workflows, integrations, and AI settings are enabled.

## Quick Example

1. Presales creates a Senior React Developer request.
2. PMO checks internal bench employees first.
3. No strong internal match is selected.
4. Recruiter opens sourcing and ranks known candidates.
5. Recruiter publishes the job and reviews new applicants.
6. Candidate applies and uploads a CV.
7. Recruiter schedules HR and technical interviews.
8. Interviewers submit scores and notes.
9. Hiring manager reviews all evidence.
10. Candidate status is updated with the final decision.

That is Talent Pilot in simple terms: one tracked hiring journey, with AI assistance and human control at every important step.
